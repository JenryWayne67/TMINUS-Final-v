T-MINUS / APOGEX v5.7.0

1. Install Node.js 24 LTS (minimum 22.13).
2. Extract the complete source or Node deployment folder.
3. Run START_GAME.bat (Windows), or sh START_GAME.sh (macOS/Linux).
   The launcher installs the pinned production dependency on its first run.
4. Open http://localhost:8095

Online Multiplayer opens /expedition.html. Four crews have sixteen human seats.
Choose opposing teams, READY UP in every client, then the host starts.
Campaign, offline bot practice, Mission Control and the original online arena remain available.

Create an account in the online lobby to keep your career across devices.
Download the recovery key and store it privately. Losing both password and recovery key
cannot be repaired through email; no email service is configured.

For multiple room workers: npm run start:cluster
For Docker, TLS and multi-host deployment, read DEPLOY.md.
Keep data/ to preserve careers and room checkpoints. Do not publish it with the client.
QA_REPORT.md states exactly what was verified and the remaining human/device field tests.
